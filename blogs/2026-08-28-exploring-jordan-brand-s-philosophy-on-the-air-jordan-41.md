Jordan Brand will soon release the Air Jordan 41 — the latest model in Michael Jordan’s OG signature shoe line. Jason Mayden, Jordan Brand’s vice president of visioneering and chief of design, took some time to chat with The Business of Fashion about the upcoming release, his philosophy around it and why the brand continues to drop a new Air Jordan shoe every year.
