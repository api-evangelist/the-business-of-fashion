Nearly two years after investigations exposed labour abuses in Italy’s luxury supply chain, brands have strengthened oversight and increased audits. But questions around pricing pressure, subcontracting and accountability remain. BoF global markets editor Sarah Kent joins The Debrief to unpack what has changed — and what hasn’t.
