The former Versace and Miu Miu designer, one of the industry’s most hotly watched voices, is set to open a new aesthetic era at Emporio Armani as well as oversee accessories at Giorgio Armani’s mainline. The appointment is the Italian fashion empire’s first creative shake-up since its founding.
